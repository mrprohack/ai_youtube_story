# The Girl Who Waited at the Last Train

## Goal
Create a 2–3 minute cinematic anime love short for YouTube/Instagram using Google Flow. Target runtime: ~2:24 (18 clips × ~8 seconds).

## Continuity Bible
- **Ren**: 19-year-old Japanese boy, slim, messy black hair, warm brown eyes, navy school-style jacket over white shirt, dark trousers, gentle reserved expression.
- **Aiko**: 19-year-old Japanese girl, long dark-brown hair with soft bangs, amber-brown eyes, cream cardigan, pale blue dress, small red ribbon in hair, calm bittersweet smile.
- **Setting**: small rural Japanese train station at night, late-summer rain, warm amber lamps, wet reflective platform, blue moonlight, quiet cinematic atmosphere.
- **Look**: premium hand-painted anime film style, emotional lighting, subtle film grain, realistic rain/wind physics, expressive eyes, clean anatomy, consistent faces and clothing, 16:9, no text, no watermark.
- **Camera**: cinematic 35mm/50mm feel, slow dolly, gentle handheld only when emotional, shallow depth of field.

## Master Style Prompt
Premium cinematic Japanese anime film, hand-painted backgrounds, expressive natural character acting, detailed wet train platform at night, warm amber practical lights contrasting cool blue moonlight, soft volumetric rain, subtle film grain, realistic cloth and hair motion, beautiful reflections, restrained romance, consistent character design, clean anatomy, no text, no watermark, 16:9.

## Story / Voiceover
Every night at exactly 11:59, Ren saw the same girl waiting alone at the last train station. She always smiled at him, but she never boarded the train. One rainy night, he finally asked why. Her answer changed everything: she was waiting for someone who had promised to return years ago. Ren began staying with her each night, sharing tea, stories, and silent moments beneath the station lights. Slowly, he stopped coming for the train. He came for her. Then one evening, Aiko gave him a folded note and asked him not to open it until midnight. When the clock struck twelve, she vanished with the arriving train’s wind. The note said: “Thank you for making my last summer feel alive again.” Ren discovered that Aiko had died at that station three years earlier, waiting for someone who never returned. The next night, Ren placed a red ribbon on the empty bench. As the final train passed, he heard her voice in the rain: “This time, I won’t wait alone.” And for the first time, Ren smiled as the last train disappeared into the night.

## Scene Plan — 18 clips

### 01 — Hook | 0:00–0:08
Wide establishing shot of an empty rural Japanese train station at 11:59 PM during light rain. Ren stands under a warm lamp. Across the platform, Aiko sits alone on a bench and looks up with a gentle smile. Slow push-in. Mysterious romantic mood.
**VO:** “Every night at exactly 11:59, Ren saw the same girl waiting alone at the last train station.”

### 02 — The Strange Routine | 0:08–0:16
Medium tracking shot as the final train arrives in rain and wind. Ren boards halfway, then looks back. Aiko remains seated, smiling, never moving toward the doors. Train lights streak across her face.
**VO:** “She always smiled at him… but she never boarded the train.”

### 03 — He Finally Asks | 0:16–0:24
Rain heavier. Ren crosses the platform holding a transparent umbrella and stops in front of Aiko. Close-up of his nervous expression, then her calm eyes.
**Dialogue:** Ren: “Why are you always here?”

### 04 — Aiko’s Answer | 0:24–0:32
Close-up. Aiko looks toward the dark tracks, fingers tightening around a small old train ticket. Wind moves her hair softly.
**Dialogue:** Aiko: “I’m waiting for someone who promised he’d come back.”

### 05 — The First Night Together | 0:32–0:40
Ren sits beside her on the bench, leaving respectful space. He offers a warm canned tea. She accepts with surprised gratitude. Rain blurs station lights behind them.
**VO:** “That night, Ren missed his train on purpose.”

### 06 — Montage: Becoming Friends | 0:40–0:48
Gentle montage in one continuous cinematic clip: different nights at the same bench, sharing snacks, laughing quietly, Ren showing her something funny on his phone while Aiko watches curiously. Keep costumes and faces consistent.
**VO:** “Then he missed it again. And again.”

### 07 — Falling in Love | 0:48–0:56
Side profile two-shot. Their shoulders almost touch. Aiko laughs, then catches Ren staring. Both look away, shy. A train rushes past behind them, lighting their faces in moving bands.
**VO:** “Somewhere between the rain and the last train… Ren stopped coming for the ride home.”

### 08 — He Comes for Her | 0:56–1:04
Ren runs up the station stairs carrying two drinks, slightly out of breath. Aiko is already there. Her face brightens instantly when she sees him. Slow-motion half-second smile, then natural speed.
**VO:** “He came for her.”

### 09 — The Red Ribbon | 1:04–1:12
Aiko removes the small red ribbon from her hair and places it in Ren’s hand. Close-up of fingers touching, emotional eye contact.
**Dialogue:** Aiko: “Keep this for me.”

### 10 — The Folded Note | 1:12–1:20
Aiko gives Ren a small folded handwritten note. The station clock shows 11:57. She looks unusually sad but tries to smile.
**Dialogue:** Aiko: “Don’t open it until midnight. Promise?”

### 11 — Final Train Approaches | 1:20–1:28
Distant headlight appears in the rain. Wind suddenly strengthens. Loose paper and Aiko’s hair move dramatically. Ren senses something wrong and steps closer.
**VO:** “That night, the last train felt different.”

### 12 — Aiko Begins to Fade | 1:28–1:36
As the train enters the station, bright light washes over Aiko. Her outline becomes faint, almost made of rain and light. Ren reaches toward her in shock.
**Dialogue:** Ren: “Aiko…?”

### 13 — Goodbye | 1:36–1:44
Emotional close-up. Aiko, partially translucent but still beautiful and calm, touches Ren’s cheek. Tears mix with rain on his face.
**Dialogue:** Aiko: “Thank you for waiting with me.”

### 14 — She Vanishes | 1:44–1:52
The train rushes between camera and characters. When it clears, Aiko is gone. The bench is empty. Ren stands frozen, clutching the folded note and red ribbon.
**Audio beat:** train sound drops into near-silence, only rain remains.

### 15 — The Note | 1:52–2:00
Close-up of Ren opening the note under the station lamp at midnight. Do not show readable generated text; show paper and his eyes reacting while narration reads it.
**VO as Aiko:** “Thank you for making my last summer feel alive again.”

### 16 — The Truth | 2:00–2:08
Ren looks at an old memorial photo/display near the station entrance and recognizes Aiko. The photo suggests she died three years earlier at this station. Keep any signage blurred/unreadable; storytelling is visual.
**VO:** “Aiko had died there three years earlier… waiting for someone who never returned.”

### 17 — Ren Returns | 2:08–2:16
Next night. Same bench, but empty. Ren gently places Aiko’s red ribbon on it, then sits beside the empty space. Rain is softer now.
**VO:** “The next night, Ren came back one last time.”

### 18 — Ending | 2:16–2:24
Final train glides away into the blue-black night. Ren hears Aiko’s faint voice, turns toward the empty bench, and smiles through tears. For one brief reflection in the wet window, Aiko appears beside him smiling, then disappears. Slow pull-back to wide station shot.
**Aiko VO:** “This time… I won’t wait alone.”
**VO:** “And for the first time, Ren smiled as the last train disappeared into the night.”

## Fast Google Flow Workflow
1. Generate Scene 01 first and choose the best Ren + Aiko appearance.
2. Save/reference the strongest frame for character continuity.
3. For every next scene, reuse the previous clip’s final frame or reference image where Flow allows it.
4. Keep the **Master Style Prompt + Continuity Bible** unchanged; append only each scene action/dialogue.
5. Generate clips at ~8 seconds. Prefer image-to-video/reference-to-video after Scene 01 rather than fresh text-to-video.
6. Use simple dialogue only on close/medium shots; add final narration/music in editing for reliability.
7. Assemble all 18 clips, trim pauses, add soft piano + rain ambience, and target 2:20–2:30 total.
